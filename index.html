4 values ✿

  1. 01curiosity

    i default to asking why a thing is the way it is before i try to change it. most bad decisions survive because nobody re-asked the question.

  2. 02empathy

    not the poster kind. the kind that shows up when you actually listen to how someone works.

  3. 03systems thinking

    a screen is never just a screen. i try to see the whole thing, the people, the data, the edge cases, so fixing one part does not quietly break three others.

  4. 04experimentation

    i would rather test a rough idea this week than defend a polished one for a month. making something is usually the fastest way to find out where it is wrong.

hi, i am saisha. ✦

i have lived in five countries so far, and every move changed how i see things a little.

india → china → indonesia → thailand → the united states

moving between them made me curious about how differently people see, use, and experience the same things. the same crosswalk, the same app, the same breakfast, understood in completely different ways. that curiosity is a big part of why i ended up in product design.

i like noticing the little things that do not work, asking why they work that way, and thinking about how they could feel different.

right now i am doing my master's in product management and design at northeastern university. mostly, though, i am a lifelong learner. i genuinely like following a question wherever it leads me.
